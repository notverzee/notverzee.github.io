─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY THE NEWS SYSTEM WORKS DIFFERENTLY HERE THAN ON stream.twechon.com
─────────────────────────────────────────────
The stream site's news system only ever remembered the 3 most recent
posts — publishing a 4th meant the 1st was gone for good. Since you
specifically asked for a tab that shows "previously published news,"
I built this version around a single growing archive file instead:
every article you ever publish stays permanently at its own /News/N/
page and stays listed on /News/ forever. The header bell and
homepage banner still only show the latest 3 — same feel as before —
but nothing is ever lost.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
THINGS WORTH KNOWING
─────────────────────────────────────────────
- Dev Mode's passcode check is client-side only (same as the stream
  site's) — it keeps casual visitors out, not a determined one.
  Anyone can view news-common.js's source and read the passcode.
- This site has no backend (GitHub Pages), so "publishing" always
  means: fill in Dev Mode → download the zip it gives you → upload
  those files over your site. There's no way around that last
  manual step without adding real server infrastructure.
- The placeholder icons (news.png, comic-icon.png, article-1.png)
  are simple generated graphics, not final art — swap them whenever
  you're ready, filenames don't need to change.
